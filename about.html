What is Outcome Memories?

Outcome Memories is a multiplayer roblox Asymetrical horror game that is inspired off of the characters of the Sonic Oddites and classic characters from the Sonic the Hedgehog franchise.

Each round, players take on the role of either a survivor or a executioner, with unique abilities. Survivors must work together to avoid the killer and escape until the final 80 seconds of the round, while the executioner must hunt every single survivor down until there is no one left.
